Help & FAQ

How the console works, and answers to common questions.

Getting started
Go to Profile, upload a photo and fill your details. Clients see this when they connect — a photo and a one-line specialty build trust fastest.
In Services, add each offering with a rate and visibility:
  • Public — appears on your share link
  • Private — only assignable to clients you choose
Hover a card and hit the camera to add cover art.
Share your invite link from Profile. Requests land under Clients → Connect requests. Once accepted, hit Assign Client a Service to put them on a plan with sessions and payment schedule.
Open any client card → View. Log sessions against the plan, mark payments, and watch the progress ring update. Your Today's Board surfaces anything pending.
FAQ
The Free plan includes 3 client slots. Upgrade from Subscription — request a tier and an admin activates it once payment is confirmed.
Messages lists every connected client with their WhatsApp number. Pick one, edit the pre-filled template ({name} is replaced automatically), and it opens in WhatsApp.
Unlogged sessions, pending payments and connect requests. The same list lives on the Notification page with your recent activity.
Settings → Appearance. Your choice is remembered on this device. There's also an animations switch if you prefer a static UI.
Tier pricing and access are configured by the platform admin. Request a switch on the Subscription page; it flips to active the moment the admin confirms payment.
